# Data rules

1. Every row cites an official source (statute, agency page or official document) with its link.
2. `verified_on` is the date a person or AI checked the source. `status`: V = verified, E = estimate or unverified.
3. Never delete history. When a rate changes, add a new row with a new `effective_from`.
4. Changes come by pull request. A maintainer checks the source before merging.
5. Public facts only. No personal data, ever.
6. Officeholders, deadlines and debt are filled only from official sources. Blank is better than a guess.
